Madsen Wood | WDD 130

My name is Madsen Wood. I am 30 years old, and have two beautiful daughters. I have worked mostly in welding and teaching/tutoring all while completing a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics. I am pursuing software development because I have enjoyed coding and with hopes that I can get consistent work that I can do at home to also take care of my daughters.

I served a mission in the Peru Lima Central Mission from June 2014 to June 2016. The people are amazing, and they have an excellent cuisine. There was a joke, though, among the missionaries that their meals consisted of a "holy trinity": rice, chicken, and potatoes. The majority of meals had some sort of combination of these three. In all seriousness, though, one of my favorite meals is called aji de gallina, which is a flavorful sauce that is served over the chicken and rice. I had heard, also, that there was a lot of asian influence, giving them an excellent menu of asian-style meals. One meal of this variety is plate of chaufa (their version of fried rice) topped with an egg omelette.
